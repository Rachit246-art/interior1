Founded in 2018 by a collective of visionary architects and preservationists, Amanthran was established to bridge ancient material reverence with sharp modern architectural minimalism.

Rather than following ephemeral trends, we practice an architectural discipline rooted in permanence: rare natural stone imported directly from Carrara and Tuscany, sustainably forested French oak aged under thermal smoke, and hand-patinated living brass that deepens in character year after year.

✦ Core Tenets ✦

Design Convictions

The Three Pillars of Spatial Grace

Our signature ethos translates high architectural principles into serene, functional sanctuaries.

♦ 01

Purity of Noble Materiality

We reject synthetic veneers and faux finishes. Every surface in an Amanthran home is honest — solid timber, monolithic volcanic stone, unadulterated marbles, and organic textured linen.

♦ 02

Architectural Proportion & Silence

We choreograph sightlines with mathematical harmony based on classical golden ratios. Acoustic buffering within walls eliminates harsh street frequencies, yielding an interior sanctuary of calm.

♦ 03

Circadian & Biophilic Light

Light is our second material. We balance expansive natural window light with concealed indirect coves that mirror the sun's natural arc, enhancing sleep hygiene and peace of mind.
