Frequently Asked Questions

Common Inquiries

Key details about our commission criteria, timelines, and architectural execution standards.

What scale of projects does Amanthran accept?

+

We specialize in full turnkey residential commissions for residences starting from 3,000 sq.ft upwards — including high-rise luxury penthouses, multi-acre coastal villas, duplexes, and heritage havelis across India.

How is the turnkey timeline managed and guaranteed?

+

Every commission is assigned a dedicated Senior Project Architect and a detailed Gantt timeline with defined milestones. We order imported stones and fabricate joinery concurrently off-site, reducing on-site construction time by up to 40%.

Do you execute projects outside Mumbai and Delhi?

+

Yes. We operate full-fledged studios in Mumbai, New Delhi, and Bengaluru, with commissioned projects completed in Goa, Hyderabad, Pune, Jaipur, Alibaug, and Chennai.

What warranties and aftercare does Amanthran provide?

+

All bespoke woodwork, stone installations, and turnkey craftsmanship carry our 10-Year Atelier Craft Warranty. In addition, we provide a 1-year complimentary biannual maintenance and material polishing check.

Can we integrate our existing heritage art and furniture?

+

Absolutely. We celebrate family heirlooms and heritage art pieces by creating tailored architectural niches, museum-grade spotlights, and harmonizing modern noble materials around them.
